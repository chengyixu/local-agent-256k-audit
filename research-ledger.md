## Selection rationale

The deployed Ornith pair is the strongest demonstrated feasible choice in this measured set for the bounded target workload, not a proven global intelligence optimum. Sparse35B/3B targets leave more practical memory/compute headroom than the measured dense alternatives. Higher-precision and alternative-engine possibilities remain research directions rather than fictional results. The public tables include negative outcomes and distinguish changing inference/client conditions.

## What is new here

The concrete client-budget failure was reproduced from actual messages, then addressed through exact backend-token admission. Schema constraints and explicit verbatim instructions were separated from the budgeting-only condition. Per-cycle timing was added after observing that provider-hook preparation precedes assistant-stream start. A privacy whitelist prevents publishing private source-bearing evidence by accident. None of these changes makes cold prefill free or guarantees factual correctness.
